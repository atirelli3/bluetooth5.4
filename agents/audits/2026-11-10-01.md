# Audit #1 — 2026-11-10

## Summary
- **Scope**: the whole repo at `main` `64128fe`: `src/bt54`, the legacy `src/demo.py`, tests + CI + hook, docs + README,
  and `agents/` + `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Method**: ponytail-audit, plus ECC agents one slice each (security-reviewer, code-reviewer, silent-failure-hunter
  with in-memory mutants, refactor-cleaner, doc-updater). Spec claims were checked against the Core v5.4 / CSS v11 HTML.
- **42 findings**: 11 HIGH, 13 MEDIUM, 17 LOW, plus 1 new research lead. The library is spec-correct (every
  Appendix D vector passes).
- **What is wrong**:
  - the demo models 5 of its scenarios wrongly (P1–P5);
  - the 2024 docs carry factual errors, mostly BR/EDR terms used for LE and the CCM order (D1–D6);
  - 3 test mutants survive (T1–T3).
- **P\* and D\*** are inputs for roadmap steps 4 (CLI) and 5 (docs): fix them there, not in the legacy files.
- **Verdicts (user)**: every row was accepted as proposed. L, T, A and D7 are fixed on `fix/audit-1`; P is the
  spec of the step-4 CLI; R1 is in the research roadmap; D goes to the step-5 rewrite. **Open**: P*, R1 and D*,
  waiting for those steps.
